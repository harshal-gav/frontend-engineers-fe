# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2026

At FrontendEngineers.com, we strive to provide the highest quality curated job listings. Because our service provides immediate access to proprietary data and digital content, we have specific policies regarding refunds and cancellations.

## 1. Subscription Cancellations

You can cancel your Pro Membership subscription at any time. Cancellations are strictly self-serve. 

**How to cancel:**
1. Log in to your account.
2. Navigate to your Account Dashboard or Billing Settings.
3. Click "Cancel Subscription" and follow the prompts.

Upon cancellation, you will retain full access to all Pro features until the end of your current paid Billing Cycle. Your subscription will not renew at the end of that cycle.

## 2. No Pro-Rated Refunds

All subscription charges are fully earned upon payment. **We do not offer pro-rated refunds** for the remainder of a billing cycle if you choose to cancel early. Your access remains active until the expiration date of your current cycle.

## 3. Exceptions & Dispute Resolution

Refunds are generally not provided, except in cases where:
- You were charged erroneously due to a technical fault on our platform.
- A duplicate charge was processed accidentally.

If you believe you were charged in error, please contact us immediately at **frontendengineersupport@gmail.com** before opening a dispute with your bank or payment provider. Fraudulent chargebacks may result in permanent bans from our platform.

## 4. Account Termination

If your account is terminated by us due to a violation of our Terms of Service (such as unauthorized data scraping or account sharing), you are not entitled to any refund for the remaining time in your billing cycle.
